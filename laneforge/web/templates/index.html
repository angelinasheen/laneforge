How the dossier answers

Core builds come from the first three legendary items each player finished, in order. The dossier answers at the most specific level whose leading build has at least 30 games, and says which level that was: your exact matchup first, then the matchup item by item, then your champion and role against every opponent. Rows are ranked by how often they were built, not by win rate; at these sample sizes a win-rate ranking would be noise, so win rate is shown with its 95% interval instead.

The situational block reads the enemy composition’s damage split, healing and crowd control. When one of those crosses a threshold it names an item class, orders the candidates by a flat-stat model, and adds a line from real games whenever your champion finished that item in at least 30 of them.
